## Design Context

- Audience: frontend developers adding sound to a web app, usually not sound designers.
- Use cases: find a sound by ear, nudge it with a slider, hear it on real controls, copy the code.
- Tone: minimal and calm. It must be obvious within seconds, with no audio jargon.
- Owner feedback (2026-11-01): layers, decibel readouts and a cue-sheet look were "very confusing". Keep plain words, few controls, and a lot of presets.
